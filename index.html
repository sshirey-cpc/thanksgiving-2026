Trip Ideas

Last year was camping at Gulf Shores State Park. Here are options for 3–4 days mid-week — within a 7–8 hour drive, or a cheap flight out of MSY.

Thanksgiving: Thu, Nov 26 Best windows: Tue–Fri or Wed–Sat Leave Tuesday AM to beat traffic

Beach camping, somewhere new

Same vibe as Gulf Shores, quieter beaches.

St. George Island / St. Joseph Peninsula

Florida Panhandle state parks
🚗 ~5.5–6 hrs
  • Wild dunes, empty late-November beaches
  • Bay-side kayaking
  • Great stargazing
Best for: the most "away from it all" feel

Topsail Hill Preserve State Park

Near Santa Rosa Beach, FL
🚗 ~4 hrs
  • Campground plus bungalows if you want a roof
  • Close to Seaside & Grayton Beach
  • Easy to grab a meal out
Best for: shortest drive, camp-or-cabin flexibility

Mountains & fall color

Cool weather, hikes, cabins, maybe the last of the leaves.

Hot Springs, AR

National park right in town
🚗 ~6.5 hrs
  • Bathhouse Row & easy hikes
  • Lake Ouachita / Lake Catherine cabins & sites
  • Garvan Gardens holiday lights (usually open by Thanksgiving)
Best for: mix of outdoors and town

Chattanooga, TN

Lookout Mountain area
🚗 ~7.5 hrs
  • Tennessee Aquarium
  • Ruby Falls & Rock City holiday lights
  • Very kid-friendly
Best for: most to do in bad weather

DeSoto State Park & Little River Canyon

Northeast Alabama
🚗 ~6.5 hrs
  • Waterfalls & canyon overlooks
  • Rustic cabins and campsites
  • Cheaper, less crowded
Best for: budget + nature

City + holiday kickoff

Christmas lights start the day after Thanksgiving.

San Antonio, TX

River Walk & Hill Country
🚗 ~8 hrs
  • River Walk lights + lighted boat parade (Fri after Thanksgiving)
  • The Alamo & the Pearl
  • Natural Bridge Caverns day trip
Best for: big holiday-season energy

Memphis, TN

Downtown + Shelby Farms
🚗 ~6 hrs
  • National Civil Rights Museum
  • Zoo Lights
  • Beale Street barbecue, Shelby Farms biking
Best for: history + food

Cheap flights from MSY

Orlando · Tampa · Fort Lauderdale

Florida springs & Gulf beaches

  • Swim/paddle the springs as a theme-park alternative
  • Flying on Thanksgiving Day is often cheapest
  • Watch out: 5 tickets + rental car often beats gas cost

Planning tips

Book soon

State park cabins and campsites fill up for Thanksgiving week, often more than a month ahead.

Keep the meal easy

Smoke a turkey breast or fry a turkey at the site, or pick up a pre-cooked dinner from a grocery store.

Beat the traffic

Leaving Tuesday morning avoids the worst of the Wednesday rush.

Drive times are approximate from New Orleans. Check park reservation sites and event dates before booking.
